Portugal D7 Visa Requirements

Passive Income Visa - Complete Document Checklist

📋 Primary Documents

  • Valid Passport Required
    Must have at least 6 months validity beyond your intended stay. Need 2 blank pages.
  • Passport Photos (2) Required
    3.5cm x 4.5cm, white background, taken within last 6 months.
  • Completed Visa Application Form Required
    Download from VFS Global or Portuguese consulate website. Fill completely, sign and date.
  • Visa Application Fee Payment Required
    Current fee approximately €90. Check VFS for accepted payment methods.

🔐 Criminal Background

  • FBI Background Check Required
    Request Identity History Summary from FBI. Processing takes 3-8 weeks.
  • Apostille for FBI Check Required
    Must be apostilled by US Department of State. Processing takes 4-8 weeks.
⏱ Start Early! FBI check + apostille can take 2-3 months total. Begin this process first.

💰 Financial Proof (D7 Specific)

  • Proof of Passive Income Required
    Pension statements, investment income, rental income, dividends. Must show regular, ongoing income.
  • Bank Statements (3-6 months) Required
    Show consistent income deposits. Minimum threshold varies (approximately €760/month base + €380 per dependent).
  • Proof of Savings Recommended
    Additional savings buffer strengthens your application.
📊 Income Threshold: Approximately 100% of Portuguese minimum wage for applicant, plus 50% for spouse, 30% per child. Check current rates.

🏥 Health Insurance

  • Travel Health Insurance Required
    Valid in Portugal, covers medical expenses, repatriation. No co-pays. Valid for visa duration.
  • Insurance Certificate/Letter Required
    Official document from insurance company stating coverage details, validity dates, and coverage territory.

🏠 Accommodation

  • Proof of Accommodation Required
    Rental agreement, property deed, or hotel booking for initial period. Must show Portuguese address.

📝 Additional Documents

  • Motivation Letter Recommended
    Brief letter explaining why you want to live in Portugal and your plans.
  • NIF (Portuguese Tax Number) Helpful
    Can be obtained before application through a fiscal representative. Speeds up banking/housing.
  • Marriage Certificate (if applicable) If Applicable
    With apostille and certified translation if applying with spouse.
  • Birth Certificates for Children If Applicable
    With apostille and certified translation if applying with minors.

✅ Final Checklist Before Appointment

  • All documents organized in order
  • Copies of all documents made
  • VFS appointment confirmation printed
  • Payment method ready
