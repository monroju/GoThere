Mexico Temporary Resident Visa

Economic Solvency Track - Complete Document Checklist

📋 Primary Documents

  • Valid Passport Required
    Must have at least 6 months validity. Need blank pages for visa sticker.
  • Passport Photos Required
    Check your specific consulate for size/background requirements. Usually recent, passport-style.
  • Completed Visa Application Form Required
    Download from your consulate's website. Some consulates have online systems.
  • Consulate Appointment Confirmation Required
    Print your appointment confirmation. Arrive early.

💰 Financial Proof - Choose ONE Option

You must prove economic solvency through EITHER bank balance OR monthly income. Requirements vary by consulate.

📊 Option A: Bank Balance Method Option A

  • Bank Statements (12 months)
    Show minimum average balance held for 12 consecutive months. Typical threshold: ~$43,000 USD (varies by consulate).
  • Account in Your Name
    Statements must clearly show your name, account number, and institution letterhead.

💵 Option B: Monthly Income Method Option B

  • Bank Statements (6 months)
    Show consistent monthly income deposits. Typical threshold: ~$2,600 USD/month (varies by consulate).
  • Proof of Income Source
    Employment letter, pay stubs, pension statements, or tax returns showing regular income.
💡 Pro Tip: Contact your specific consulate for exact thresholds. Requirements can vary significantly between consulates and change periodically.

📄 Supporting Documents (Check Consulate Requirements)

  • Investment Statements (if using)
    Brokerage accounts can supplement bank accounts. Get official statements.
  • Employment Verification Letter (if employed)
    Company letterhead, stating position, salary, and employment duration.
  • Tax Returns (optional but helpful)
    Can strengthen application by showing income history.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Documents (If Applicable)

  • Marriage Certificate
    If applying with spouse. May need apostille and/or translation depending on consulate.
  • Birth Certificates for Children
    If applying with minor children. May need apostille and/or translation.
  • Consent from Non-Traveling Parent
    If only one parent is applying with children, notarized consent from other parent.
⚠️ Important Timeline: After receiving your visa sticker, you have 180 days to enter Mexico. Once in Mexico, you have 30 days to exchange your visa at INM (immigration office) for your actual resident card. Don't miss this deadline!

🛫 Post-Approval: INM Exchange

After entering Mexico, you'll need these for your INM appointment:

  • Passport with Visa Sticker
  • FMM Form (stamped at entry)
    Do NOT lose this! You receive it when entering Mexico.
  • INM Fee Payment Receipt
    Pay at a Mexican bank before your appointment. Keep the receipt.
  • Proof of Mexican Address
    Utility bill, rental agreement, or bank statement showing Mexican address.
  • Photos (INM specifications)
    Check INM for exact photo requirements.

✅ Pre-Appointment Checklist

  • Confirmed appointment date and time
  • All documents organized and in order
  • Copies of all documents made
  • Visa fee payment method ready
  • Consulate address and parking/transit plan
