Delete Your Data

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. You may request the deletion of all data associated with your GoThere account at any time.

What Data Gets Deleted

When you submit a deletion request, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account information — your email address and authentication credentials (Firebase Authentication)
  • Uploaded documents — all PDF files and associated metadata you stored in the app (Firebase Storage)
  • App preferences — saved settings, task progress, and any personalization data

Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to the address below with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address associated with your GoThere account so we can locate your data.
  3. We will process your request and permanently delete all associated data within 30 days.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.
Request Data Deletion

Or email us directly at: gabriel@getgothere.app

Data Retention After Deletion

After processing your request, all personal data is permanently deleted. We do not retain any copies unless required by law for fraud prevention or legal compliance purposes.
