1) Overview

GoThere: Relocation Guide helps users organize relocation tasks and store personal documents related to moving. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

2) Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to provide core app functionality:

A. Account Information (Firebase Authentication)

  • Email address (required). Used to create and manage your user account, sign you in, and help secure access to your data.

B. User Content You Upload (Firebase Storage)

  • Documents you choose to upload, including PDF files and related file metadata (such as file name, upload time, and size). These documents are stored so you can access them later inside the app.

C. Basic App Data

  • Depending on your device and Firebase configuration, we may collect limited technical information such as app version, device type, and basic diagnostic logs to improve stability and fix bugs.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive data like government ID numbers, health records, or payment card details unless you choose to place such information inside a document you upload.

3) How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide account access and authentication
  • Store and retrieve your uploaded documents
  • Maintain app functionality and improve reliability (for example, bug fixing)
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

4) Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We share data only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers: The app uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Storage) to operate core features.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or valid legal process.
  • Protection and safety: To protect users, the public, and the service from harm or abuse.

5) Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Your data is stored using Google Firebase services, which use industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit and encryption at rest where supported by the platform and configuration.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and limit access.

6) Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Uploaded documents remain stored until you delete them within the app (if that feature exists) or request deletion.

If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify your personal information and stored documents within a reasonable timeframe, unless we are required to keep certain information for legal reasons.

7) Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of all personal data associated with your GoThere account. Upon request, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account information (email address and authentication credentials)
  • All uploaded documents and associated metadata
  • App preferences, task progress, and personalization data

To request deletion, email us at gabriel@getgothere.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation once complete.

Request Data Deletion

Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. We do not retain copies unless required by law.

8) Your Choices and Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated stored documents by contacting us at gabriel@getgothere.app, or by visiting our Data Deletion page.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws (such as access, correction, deletion, or portability). We will honor applicable requests as required.

9) Children's Privacy

GoThere: Relocation Guide is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at gabriel@getgothere.app and we will take steps to delete it.

10) International Data Transfers

Firebase may process and store data on servers located outside your country. By using the app, you understand that your information may be transferred and processed in other jurisdictions.
